# Mayes Maths & More

Free maths practice for children in the UK, from Year 1 to GCSE, plus 11+ practice, with Pip the owl as tutor.

- **Free to download, and free thereafter.** No in-app purchases. No ads. No accounts.
- **Works without the internet** once installed. Nothing about your child leaves the device.
- Built on the National Curriculum in England (mathematics programmes of study).

**Use it:** https://nsmayes.github.io/Mayes-Maths-More/

This repository holds the published app (a website that installs as an app). See the About page in the app for credits and licences.
