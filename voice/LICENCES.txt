Learn with Pip - the natural offline voice ("Cori")

Everything in this folder is free to use. It was built by tools/build-voice.py from these parts:

1. The voice: Piper voice "en_GB-cori-medium" by Bryce Beattie
   Licence: public domain. It was trained from scratch on public-domain LibriVox recordings.
   Source: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori/medium
   Author's page: https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/
   (Most other British Piper voices were deliberately not used. They are fine-tuned from the "lessac" voice, whose
   recordings are licensed for research only.)

2. ONNX Runtime Web 1.18.0, which runs the voice model
   Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation.
   Source: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

3. piper_phonemize, which turns words into speech sounds (WebAssembly build from @diffusionstudio/piper-wasm 1.0.0)
   Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) Michael Hansen. Source: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper-phonemize
   WebAssembly build: https://github.com/diffusionstudio/piper-wasm

4. eSpeak NG (inside piper_phonemize) and its English data
   Licence: GNU General Public License version 3 (GPL-3.0). It is free software. You may use, copy and share it.
   If you pass the app on to others, you must also let them have the source code. It is available at
   https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
   The data was trimmed to English only; nothing else was changed.

The Piper speech engine itself (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) is MIT-licensed.
