About the app

Mayes Maths & More is free maths practice for children in England, from Year 1 to GCSE, plus 11+ preparation. It runs entirely in the browser on your device, and works offline once installed. There are no accounts, adverts or tracking, and children’s progress never leaves the device. See the privacy note.

The Mayes Maths & More name, logo, characters and questions are © 2026 Neil Mayes. They are free to use for learning at home and at school.

Every answer is checked by an automated checker that works each one out a second way, but no check is perfect. If a question looks wrong, children can tap “Something wrong with this question?” and grown-ups can look at it in the Grown-ups’ area.

Curriculum and exams

National Curriculum in England
The curriculum statements shown in the app are taken from the Department for Education’s mathematics programmes of study and GCSE subject content. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
Practice papers
Practice papers are written for this app in the style of the KS2 SATs and of the AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR and WJEC Eduqas GCSE papers. They are not official papers, and the app is not endorsed by, or connected with, the Standards and Testing Agency or any exam board. The exam boards’ names are trademarks of their owners.
11+
11+ practice follows the common multiple-choice style. It is not an official test and is not connected with GL Assessment, CEM or any school.
